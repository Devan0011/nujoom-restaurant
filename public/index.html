About Nujoom Biriyani House

15+ Years

A Legacy of Authentic Flavors

Nujoom Biriyani House has been serving the finest biriyanis in Palakkad for over 15 years. Our journey began with a simple vision - to bring the authentic taste of Hyderabadi and Malabar cuisine to the heart of Kerala.

Every biriyani we prepare is a labor of love, using the finest ingredients: premium basmati rice, hand-selected spices, and the freshest meats.
